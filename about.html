Our Story & Background

Pune’s Specialist in Non-Destructive Leakage Solutions

Founded in 2010 in Pune, MY Waterproofing was created with a single uncompromising mission: to give homeowners, housing societies, and commercial property owners a scientific, durable leakage solution that never requires demolishing good tiles or destroying expensive interior decor.

For decades, conventional contractors gave Pune homeowners only one costly option: break open bathroom floors, strip off expensive tiles, create days of cement dust, and replace the sand bed — only for the leakage to reappear in 6 to 12 months. We revolutionized this process with calibrated mechanical pressure injection grouting using specialized waterproofing compound.

By injecting polymer-modified waterproofing compound directly into the micro-voids, cold joints, and honeycombs inside the structural concrete slab under controlled pressure, we seal water pathways at the source. The entire job is completed within 24 to 48 hours, leaving your bathrooms, terraces, and walls completely intact and dry.

Our 4 Core Engineering Standards

Every project we undertake adheres to strict quality controls, standardized safety procedures, and proven non-destructive methodologies.

🛡️

100% Non-Destructive

We preserve your expensive bathroom tiles, vitrified flooring, and designer sanitaryware. We access concealed voids through tiny grout joints that are invisibly resealed.

🔬

Root-Cause Diagnosis

No superficial patch work. We identify whether moisture is traveling from pipe penetrations, expansion joints, or porous slab honeycombs before proposing a solution.

💧

Waterproofing Compound

We use specialized polymer waterproofing compound formulations engineered for hydrostatic pressure resistance, concrete elasticity, and long-term durability.

⏱️

Fast 24-48 Hr Turnaround

Most residential projects are completed within 1 to 2 days without mess, demolition noise, or disruption to your family's daily routine.
