Company Profile

Pune’s Specialist in Leakage Repair & Waterproofing

MY Waterproofing is a professional waterproofing and leakage repair service provider based in Pune, Maharashtra. We specialize in identifying and resolving water leakage, seepage, dampness, and crack-related problems in residential and commercial properties.

Our aim is to provide reliable, durable, and cost-effective waterproofing solutions using suitable materials and application techniques. From bathroom leakage to terrace waterproofing, water tank treatment, and external wall crack repairs, we offer solutions tailored to the condition of each property.

We focus on quality workmanship, proper surface preparation, and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

"To provide dependable, engineering-grade waterproofing and leakage repair solutions that safeguard buildings from water intrusion, seepage, and structural dampness across Pune, Maharashtra. We deliver transparent diagnoses, professional surface preparation, and long-term protection tailored to each property without disruptive, unnecessary destruction."
