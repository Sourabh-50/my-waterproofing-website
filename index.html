Bathroom Leakage

Bathroom & Toilet Floor Slab Seepage (No Tile Removal)

Sub-floor water accumulation from faulty tile grouting, or unsealed trap collars leaks directly to the neighbor’s ceiling below or adjacent bedroom walls.

Common Warning Signs:

Paint peeling on adjoining bedroom or hallway skirting boards
Lower floor flat complaining of dripping water from bathroom ceiling slab
Persistent foul musty odour and black mould growth in grout crevices
Recommended Solution: Pressure injection grouting with waterproofing compound directly into concrete voids and joint sealing—100% no tile breaking required.

Client Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about our waterproofing methods, non-destructive grouting, and inspection process in Pune.

Can bathroom and toilet leakage be repaired without breaking tiles?

Yes. Through specialized pressure injection grouting, we inject waterproofing compound directly into capillary voids, sunken slab cracks, and joints. This seals the leak path effectively while keeping your existing bathroom and floor tiles intact.

What services do you provide for terrace and roof waterproofing?

Our terrace and roof solutions include thorough surface preparation, crack filling, joint sealing, and applying protective elastomeric waterproof coatings tailored to the condition of the terrace to prevent monsoon rainwater seepage.

Do you provide waterproofing for overhead water tanks?

Yes. We treat concrete overhead water tank leakages, apply internal waterproof coatings, and treat cracks and joints using safe, potable-water compliant waterproofing systems.

Which areas in Pune do you provide waterproofing services to?

We provide services across all major localities of Pune and PCMC, including Kothrud, Baner, Balewadi, Wakad, Hinjawadi, Aundh, Pashan, Bavdhan, Viman Nagar, Kharadi, Kalyani Nagar, Koregaon Park, Magarpatta City, Amanora Park Town, Hadapsar, Pimple Saudagar, Pimple Nilakh, Ravet, Wanowrie, NIBM Road, Undri, Kondhwa, Sinhagad Road, Dhayari, and other areas across Pune.

Do you handle both residential and commercial properties?

Yes. We offer waterproofing and leakage repair solutions tailored to the condition of each property, covering residential apartments, independent homes, housing societies, and commercial structures.
